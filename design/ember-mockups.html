02Tile anatomy & mechanics WHY THE BOARD NEVER SITS STILL
TILE ANATOMY · PLAYER GRADIENT
●●●  Burn pips
Every tile lives 3 rounds, then collapses to ash. The board never sits still — no defensive camping.
✦  Ash cells
Burned-out hexes become wildcards. Dead words become new openings.
×3  Forge hex
The bonus cell migrates each round toward the coldest region of the board, pulling play around.
⚖  Plead a word
Rejected slang? Argue your case in one line — an AI judge rules live, once per match.
⬡  Three axes
Hex board, radius 3 to start — words read along three directions instead of two.
⏱  Simultaneous rounds
Both players place at once in 60-second rounds. No async waiting, ever.
